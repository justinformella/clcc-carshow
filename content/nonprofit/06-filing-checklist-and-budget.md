# Filing Checklist & Projected Budget
## Crystal Lake Cars and Caffeine NFP

## Checklist (in order)

- [ ] **1. Board approves this packet** — articles, bylaws, conflict of interest policy. (Today, in the room.)
- [ ] **2. File Illinois Articles of Incorporation** (Form NFP-102.10) — mail to IL Secretary of State, Dept. of Business Services, 501 S. Second St., Room 350, Springfield, IL 62756, or online. **Fee: $50** (mail). Justin is paying.
- [ ] **3. Get EIN** — free at irs.gov (search "apply for EIN online"), ~10 minutes. Needed for everything after this.
- [ ] **4. Hold organizational meeting** — adopt bylaws, elect officers, sign COI disclosures. Use `04-organizational-meeting-minutes.md`.
- [ ] **5. Open bank account** — bring EIN letter + filed articles + signed minutes. Treasurer + President as signers.
- [ ] **6. File IRS Form 1023-EZ** on Pay.gov. **Fee: $275.** See `05-form-1023-ez-filing-guide.md`. Do this within 27 months of the Illinois file-stamp date.
- [ ] **7. Register with IL Attorney General** (Form CO-1, Charitable Trust Bureau). **Fee: $15.** Do this BEFORE soliciting sponsors or donations for the next show.
- [ ] **8. Save the IRS determination letter** when it arrives — scan it, keep the original.

**Total government fees: ~$340.** No accountant required.

## Ongoing compliance (calendar it)

| What | When | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| IRS Form 990-N (e-postcard) | Yearly, by May 15 (for calendar-year filers) | Free |
| Illinois annual report (Secretary of State) | Yearly | ~$61 |
| IL Attorney General annual report (AG990-IL) | Yearly | $15 |
| Board annual meeting + elections | Each January | — |
| COI disclosure statements | Each January | — |

Miss the 990 three years running and the IRS auto-revokes the exemption. Don't.
