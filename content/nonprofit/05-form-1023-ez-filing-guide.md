## Attestations (the EZ is mostly checkboxes)

You will attest that: the organizing documents contain the required purpose, inurement, and dissolution clauses; the board adopted bylaws and a conflict of interest policy; annual gross receipts and assets are within limits; and the organization will file annual Form 990 returns.

## After submission

- Pay.gov issues a confirmation number — **save it**.
- Typical processing: **2–4 weeks** (longer if the IRS asks follow-up questions).
- The determination letter arrives by mail — this is the proof of 501(c)(3) status. Scan it and keep the original with corporate records.
- You may operate, accept donations, and sign sponsor agreements while the application is pending. Donation receipts should note "Federal 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status pending" until the letter arrives; once approved within the 27-month window, deductibility is retroactive to the incorporation date.

## Common trip-ups to avoid

1. **File the EIN first.** The EZ requires it; EINs are free and instant at irs.gov (don't pay a third-party site).
2. **Don't solicit before Illinois charity registration** (Form CO-1, $15, IL Attorney General) — soliciting first triggers a $200 late fee.
3. **Entry fees aren't deductible donations.** Vehicle registration fees buy admission to the show (quid pro quo) — receipts must say so. Pure donations and sponsorships above fair-market value of any perks are deductible.
4. **Keep the determination letter.** Grantmakers, the state, and banks will ask for it for years.
