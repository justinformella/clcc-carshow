# Bylaws — DRAFT
## Crystal Lake Cars and Caffeine NFP

> **Status:** DRAFT — to be adopted by the Board of Directors at the organizational meeting.

---

## Article I — Name and Offices

**Section 1. Name.** The name of the corporation is Crystal Lake Cars and Caffeine NFP (the "Corporation"), an Illinois General Not For Profit Corporation.

**Section 2. Principal Office.** The principal office of the Corporation shall be located at 125 Erick Street, Suite A115, Crystal Lake, IL 60014, or at such other place as the Board of Directors may designate.

**Section 3. Registered Office and Agent.** The Corporation shall maintain a registered office and registered agent in the State of Illinois as required by law. The Board may change the registered office or agent at any time.

## Article II — Purpose

The Corporation is organized exclusively for charitable purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, as set forth in its Articles of Incorporation, including organizing an annual charitable car show and related fundraising events benefiting the Crystal Lake Food Pantry, Inc.

## Article III — Membership

The Corporation shall have no members. All corporate powers shall be exercised by the Board of Directors.

## Article IV — Board of Directors

**Section 1. General Powers.** The affairs of the Corporation shall be managed by its Board of Directors.

**Section 2. Number.** The Board shall consist of no fewer than three (3) and no more than seven (7) directors. The initial Board shall have four (4) directors.

**Section 3. Qualifications.** Directors shall be individuals at least 18 years of age who support the charitable purposes of the Corporation.

**Section 4. Election and Term.** Directors shall be elected at the annual meeting of the Board and shall serve one-year terms, beginning immediately upon election and ending at the next annual meeting or until a successor is elected and qualified. Directors may serve consecutive terms without limit unless the Board adopts term limits by resolution.

**Section 5. Vacancies.** Any vacancy on the Board, including a vacancy created by an increase in the number of directors, may be filled by a majority vote of the remaining directors, even if less than a quorum. A director elected to fill a vacancy shall serve for the remainder of the unexpired term.

**Section 6. Removal.** Any director may be removed, with or without cause, by a two-thirds vote of the directors then in office.

**Section 7. Resignation.** Any director may resign at any time by giving written notice to the President or Secretary. If a resignation would reduce the Board below three directors, the Board shall elect a replacement promptly to maintain the statutory minimum.

## Article V — Meetings of the Board

**Section 1. Annual Meeting.** The annual meeting of the Board shall be held each January, at a time and place designated by the President, for the election of directors and officers and the transaction of other business.

**Section 2. Regular and Special Meetings.** Regular meetings may be scheduled by the Board. Special meetings may be called by the President or by any two directors, upon at least five (5) days' notice.

**Section 3. Notice.** Notice of meetings shall be given by mail, email, or telephone at least five (5) days before the meeting, stating the time, place, and purpose.

**Section 4. Quorum and Voting.** A majority of the directors then in office shall constitute a quorum. The act of a majority of the directors present at a meeting at which a quorum is present shall be the act of the Board.

**Section 5. Tie Votes.** In the event of a tie vote of the Board, the President shall cast the deciding vote.

**Section 6. Action Without Meeting.** Any action required or permitted at a meeting of the Board may be taken without a meeting if all directors consent in writing (including by email).

**Section 7. Remote Participation.** Directors may participate in meetings by telephone or video conference, and such participation shall constitute presence in person.

**Section 8. Minutes.** The Secretary shall keep minutes of all Board meetings, recording attendance, motions, votes, and any recusals under the Conflict of Interest Policy.

## Article VI — Officers

**Section 1. Officers.** The officers of the Corporation shall be a President, a Secretary, and a Treasurer. The Board may also elect a Vice President and such other officers as it deems appropriate. One person may not hold the offices of President and Secretary simultaneously.

**Section 2. Election and Term.** Officers shall be elected annually by the Board at the annual meeting and shall serve one-year terms, or until a successor is elected and qualified. Officers may be re-elected without limit.

**Section 3. President.** The President shall be the chief executive officer of the Corporation, shall preside at all meetings of the Board, shall cast the deciding vote in the event of a tie, and shall have general supervision of the affairs of the Corporation. The President may sign contracts and other instruments authorized by the Board.

**Section 4. Secretary.** The Secretary shall keep the minutes of Board meetings, maintain the corporate records and seal (if any), give notice of meetings, and certify corporate documents as needed for filings.

**Section 5. Treasurer.** The Treasurer shall have custody of the corporate funds, keep accurate financial records, present financial reports to the Board at least annually, oversee the annual information return (IRS Form 990-N or successor), and ensure state filings remain current. The Treasurer shall disburse funds only as authorized by the Board or the approved budget.

**Section 6. Vacancies and Removal.** Officer vacancies shall be filled by the Board. Any officer may be removed, with or without cause, by a two-thirds vote of the directors then in office.

## Article VII — Committees

The Board may establish committees (including an event committee for the annual car show) by resolution, defining their membership, authority, and reporting duties. Committees serve at the pleasure of the Board.

## Article VIII — Conflict of Interest

The Corporation shall maintain a written Conflict of Interest Policy, reviewed annually by the Board. Each director and officer shall sign an annual disclosure statement. Any director with a direct or indirect interest in a transaction before the Board shall disclose the interest and recuse from discussion and voting on the matter, and the recusal shall be recorded in the minutes.

## Article IX — Financial Matters

**Section 1. Fiscal Year.** The fiscal year of the Corporation shall be the calendar year, January 1 through December 31.

**Section 2. Budget.** The Board shall approve an annual budget before the start of each fiscal year.

**Section 3. Grants.** All grants and distributions (including the annual distribution of net proceeds to the Crystal Lake Food Pantry, Inc.) shall be authorized by the Board and documented in the minutes.

**Section 4. Compensation.** Directors shall serve without compensation. Officers shall serve without compensation unless the Board expressly authorizes reasonable compensation, subject to the Conflict of Interest Policy.

**Section 5. Loans.** No loans shall be made by the Corporation to any director or officer.

**Section 6. Checks and Disbursements.** All checks, drafts, and disbursements shall be signed or authorized as the Board directs by resolution. Any disbursement above an amount set by Board resolution shall require two authorized signatures.

## Article X — Indemnification

To the fullest extent permitted by Illinois law, the Corporation shall indemnify any director or officer against expenses, judgments, and amounts paid in settlement in connection with any proceeding arising from their service to the Corporation, provided they acted in good faith and in a manner reasonably believed to be in the best interests of the Corporation.

## Article XI — Amendments

These Bylaws may be amended or repealed by a two-thirds vote of the directors then in office at any meeting at which a quorum is present, provided the proposed amendment was included in the meeting notice.

## Article XII — Dissolution

Upon dissolution, the Corporation's assets shall be distributed as provided in the Articles of Incorporation and in compliance with Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
